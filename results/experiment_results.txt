CIC/EMBER common evaluation setup
---------------------------------
- Binary labels: 0 = benign, 1 = malware.
- Balanced sample: 100 benign + 100 malware records.
- Primary split: 140 training + 60 testing records (70/30), random_state=42,
  stratified. The CIC 80/20 reproduction is listed separately.
- Preprocessing is fitted on training data only: median imputation, standard
  scaling, PCA to 4 components, then feature-range scaling.
- Baseline quantum model: exact FidelityQuantumKernel with a 4-qubit
  ZZFeatureMap, reps=2, linear entanglement, feature range [-1, 1], and a
  precomputed-kernel SVC with C=1.
- Tuned variants were selected only with five-fold stratified CV on the 140-row
  outer training split. The 60-row outer test was evaluated after selection.

Interpretation and hardware caveats
-----------------------------------
- These are single small outer-test splits, not final population estimates.
- The outer test records had already appeared in project results, so they are
  not pristine at the project level even though tuning did not use them.
- The tuning CV treats the PCA representation fitted on all outer-training rows
  as fixed. A publication-grade nested estimate should refit preprocessing in
  every inner fold.
- Exact simulator kernels are probability fidelities. Hardware kernels will be
  affected by finite shots, transpilation, readout/gate noise, calibration time,
  and backend topology. Record backend, qubit layout, job IDs, shots, circuit
  depth/two-qubit count, mitigation, and run timestamps for every comparison.
- Fabiana's 200/100 kernel needs about 39,900 nontrivial symmetric train/test
  fidelity evaluations before shots. Use the compact matched hardware protocol
  first; do not submit this full matrix as an initial hardware job.
- For CIC and EMBER, take only the declared baseline and CV-selected circuits
  into hardware comparison. Do not rerun the complete 32-candidate selection
  grid on the QPU; selection has already been performed on exact training data.
